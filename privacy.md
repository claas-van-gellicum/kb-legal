# Privacy policy

Gmail MCP is used only by its developer with his own Google accounts. It reads his own Gmail and Google Calendar (read-only) and keeps copies in his private notes. Nothing is shared, sold or used to train AI models.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.
